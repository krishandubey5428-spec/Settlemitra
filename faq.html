FAQs

Questions Clients Ask Most Often

Clear answers to help you understand debt settlement and Settlemitra’s support model.

What is debt settlement?

Debt settlement is a process where an agreed amount lower than the full outstanding is negotiated for closure, subject to creditor approval.

Can settlement reduce my total payable amount?

It may, depending on your debt profile, creditor policy, and negotiation context. Outcomes vary by case.

How long does the process usually take?

Timelines vary by number of creditors, debt amount, and responsiveness. Many cases move in phased milestones over several months.

Do you guarantee settlement results?

No ethical debt settlement firm should promise guaranteed outcomes. We provide strategic, transparent support to improve outcomes.

Will my personal details stay confidential?

Yes. We treat client information with strict confidentiality and process only what is needed for consultation and support.

How do I start with Settlemitra?

Submit your details on the contact page and our team will schedule a free initial consultation.

How is debt settlement different from a debt consolidation loan?

A debt consolidation loan combines multiple debts into one new loan, which you still need to repay in full. Debt settlement, on the other hand, involves negotiating with creditors to accept a reduced lump-sum payment to close the account — meaning you may pay less than the total amount owed.

Will debt settlement affect my credit score?

Yes, debt settlement can temporarily impact your credit score. However, for many people already experiencing financial stress, settlement offers a structured path to resolution. Over time, with responsible financial behavior, credit scores typically recover.

What types of debts can be settled?

Debt settlement is typically applicable to unsecured debts such as credit card balances, personal loans, and business loans. Secured debts like home loans or vehicle loans are generally handled differently.

How much can I save through debt settlement?

Savings vary based on your debt profile, creditor policies, and negotiation context. While outcomes are not guaranteed, settlements can sometimes reduce the payable amount significantly compared to the total outstanding balance.

Is debt settlement legal in India?

Yes, debt settlement is a legal process in India. Creditors are free to accept negotiated settlements as full closure of an account. Settlemitra's approach is fully compliant with applicable laws and regulations.

What documents do I need to get started?

Typically, you will need recent bank statements, loan account statements, credit card statements, identity proof (Aadhaar/PAN), and a summary of your monthly income and expenses. Our team will guide you through the exact requirements during your initial consultation.

How does Settlemitra charge for its services?

We offer a free initial consultation. Our fee structure is transparent and performance-oriented. Detailed fee information is discussed during the consultation based on your specific case.

What if a creditor refuses to settle?

Not all creditors settle in every case. If a creditor declines, we review alternative strategies including restructuring, hardship plans, or other resolution paths. We work persistently on your behalf while keeping your options open.
